## What does Regulation 261/2004 provide?

Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 gives passengers rights when a flight is cancelled, badly delayed or boarding is denied. According to the Council of the EU and the EUR-Lex summary, compensation comes in three tiers:

| Distance | Compensation |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,500 km | 250 euros |
| Intra-EU over 1,500 km, and other flights between 1,500 and 3,500 km | 400 euros |
| All other flights | 600 euros |

Compensation applies to a delay of more than 3 hours on arrival (according to EU Court of Justice case law), to a cancellation announced less than 14 days before departure and to denied boarding. It is not due where the cause is an extraordinary circumstance.

## Are flights to and from Albania covered?

This is the question Albanian passengers ask most, and the answer depends on Article 3(1) of the regulation. According to the text, the regulation applies to:

- **(a)** passengers departing from an airport in an EU Member State, on any airline;
- **(b)** passengers flying from an airport in a non-Member State to an airport in the EU, only if the operating airline is an EU airline and the passenger did not receive benefits or compensation in the third country.

From this text, these situations follow:

| Flight | What the text says |
|---|---|
| From an EU airport to Tirana, on any airline | Covered under (a) |
| From Tirana to the EU, on an EU airline | May be covered under (b); we found no separate Commission confirmation for Albania |
| From Tirana to the EU, on a non-EU airline | Not covered under (a) or (b) |
| From Tirana to a non-EU country | Not covered under (a) or (b) |

So on a return trip, the leg back from an EU airport to Tirana is covered, while the leg departing from Tirana depends on the airline and on how Article 3(1)(b) is read. We did not confirm whether Albanian law gives similar rights; Tirana airport has a passenger rights page, which we link in the sources, but we have not read it in detail.

## What changes with the reform?

The Council and the European Parliament reached a political agreement on 15 June 2026 on stronger passenger rights rules. According to the sources we read, the compensation tiers are kept, procedural rules are added, and the rules apply 12 months after publication in the Official Journal. The final approval status was not confirmed here, so we cannot present the new rules as in force.

## What to do if your flight is delayed or cancelled

1. Keep your ticket, booking confirmation and boarding pass.
2. Ask in writing for the reason for the delay or cancellation and your actual arrival time.
3. Keep receipts for reasonable expenses you incurred (food, accommodation), if any.
4. Send your claim to the airline and note the date you sent it.
5. Check whether your flight is covered using the tables above, and read the airport’s passenger rights page.
6. If you booked through an agency, keep its confirmation too and, for questions about your case, [write to us about your request](/en/#kerkese).
