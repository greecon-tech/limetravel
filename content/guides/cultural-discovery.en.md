## What is cultural discovery in Albania?

Cultural discovery is a way of getting to know Albania through its old towns, castles and archaeological sites. The three best-known places are Berat and Gjirokastër, two historic centres that are part of the UNESCO World Heritage List, and Butrint, on the southern coast.

## Berat: the town of the castle and the icons

Berat Castle is a 13th-century fortress, according to the Albanian tourism agency, with Byzantine churches inside it. It also houses the Onufri National Iconographic Museum. Below the castle lie the Mangalem and Gorica quarters, with cobbled lanes and white facades.

## Gjirokastër: the town of stone

Gjirokastër is known for its stone tower houses, mostly from the 17th to 19th centuries, for the Old Bazaar and for the castle above the town, with views over the Drino valley. The Ethnographic Museum displays old furniture, costumes and tools.

## Butrint: a microcosm of the Mediterranean

Butrint is a World Heritage site and national park. The tourism agency describes it as a place with structures from Hellenic temples to an Ottoman fort. Sources disagree on the year of inscription and the park’s area, so we mention it only as a UNESCO site. We could not confirm opening hours or ticket prices, so check locally.

## How do you combine them?

Berat and Gjirokastër are in the centre and south of the country; Butrint is on the coast, near Sarandë. Distances are long: about 157–160 km between Berat and Gjirokastër, according to route calculators. For that reason we recommend at least 3 days. Idea itineraries are on the pages for [Berat and Gjirokastër](/en/destinations/berat-gjirokaster-heritage-towns) and the [Albanian Riviera](/en/destinations/albanian-riviera-ionian-coast).

## When is the best time to go?

Guides usually recommend spring and autumn. Summer is hot and crowded. In winter some businesses outside the cities close.

## Practical tips

The old towns have cobbles and slopes, so wear suitable shoes. Keep in mind that opening times and entry prices may change and we did not find them published officially.
