## What is the Albanian Riviera?

The Albanian Riviera is the Ionian coast of southern Albania. According to the Albanian tourism agency it runs from Vlorë to Ksamil, passing Llogara National Park, Dhërmi, Himara and Sarandë. Sarandë is described as the city facing Corfu and the gateway to Butrint and the Blue Eye.

## The main places

- **Llogara:** a national park in the Ceraunian Mountains, established in 1966, with pine forests. The SH8 road crosses the Llogara Pass; a tunnel of about 6 km opened in July 2024 and bypasses it, according to encyclopaedic sources.
- **Dhërmi and Himara:** the old village of Dhërmi sits above the sea, with the churches of St Mary and St Stephen. Himara has a castle, the church of St Spyridon and the ruins of ancient Himara; Porto Palermo Castle is about 8 km to the south.
- **Sarandë and Ksamil:** Ksamil is one of the most sought-after summer destinations; the small islands are reached by boat.
- **Butrint:** a UNESCO World Heritage site and national park. The tourism agency describes it as a microcosm of the Mediterranean, with structures from Hellenic temples to an Ottoman fort. Sources disagree on the year of inscription and the park’s area, so we mention it only as a UNESCO site.
- **The Blue Eye:** a natural monument near the village of Muzinë, about 18 km from Sarandë, where the spring rises from a depth of over 45 m according to the tourism agency.

## How do you get there from Tirana?

The main road is the SH8 from Fier towards Vlorë, Llogara, Dhërmi, Himara and Sarandë, about 126 km according to encyclopaedic sources. An unofficial blog gives about 155 km and about 2 hours from Tirana to Vlorë; we could not confirm full distances officially. Vlorë airport has no confirmed scheduled service, so in practice travellers use Tirana.

From Sarandë there are ferries to Corfu. More than one company operates and timetables change by season, so check the operator before you plan.

## When is the best time to go?

Beaches are busiest from June to September. One guide suggests September to December for good weather and fewer visitors, while Himara and Dhërmi are quieter. We could not confirm sea temperatures from an official source, so do not assume the sea is warm outside summer.

## Practicalities and safety

- Beach rules change: a 2025 Council of Ministers regulation reserves 30% of every 1,000 m of coastline for free public use, but the State Audit reported in April 2025 that it was not fully applied. Ask in advance about sunbed prices.
- The UK government’s advice for Albania urges care when driving, warns of wildfires from April to October, and advises using only licensed centres for boats and jet skis.
- The emergency number is 112.
