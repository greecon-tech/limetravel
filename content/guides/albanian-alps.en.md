## What are Theth and Valbona?

Theth and Valbona are two valleys in the Albanian Alps, in the north of the country. According to Albania’s National Agency of Protected Areas (AKZM), the Alps National Park was declared on 26 January 2022 by Council of Ministers decision no. 59 and merged the former Theth and Valbona Valley national parks together with the Gashi River area. The park covers about 82,845 hectares. The highest peak in the mountains is Jezerca at 2,694 m, and AKZM mentions about 30 glacial lakes.

Theth is known for its small mountain village, the Lock-in Tower and its church. The Albanian tourism agency describes Theth as a historic centre. Valbona lies further east, in the next valley, and is usually linked to Theth by the mountain crossing.

## How do you get there from Tirana?

The nearest airport is Tirana. The road to Theth usually passes through Shkodër, Koplik, Boga and the Thorë pass, then the SH21 to the village. The Shkodër–Theth section is described as narrow and winding; reported journey times range from 1.5 to 3 hours depending on how you travel. The condition of the road surface differs between sources, so check before you leave, especially in bad weather. We could not confirm the total distance and time from Tirana to Theth from an official source.

For Valbona, many travellers use Lake Koman and the ferry connection; we could not confirm the ferry timetable, so ask the operator.

## When is the best time to go?

Guidebook sources suggest that the most suitable season for hiking is roughly June to September, sometimes into early October. Outside that period there is a risk of snow and many guesthouses and cafés close. The road to Theth can be blocked by snow in winter.

## What can you see and do?

- The Lock-in Tower in Theth, described by the tourism agency as a monument linked to the blood-feud tradition.
- Short valley walks, such as the one to the Blue Eye of Theth, about 1 hour according to Wikivoyage.
- The Theth–Valbona crossing, the best-known hike in the area.
- Staying in family guesthouses, where local food is usually served.

## The Theth–Valbona hike: what to know

Figures vary by source and starting point: about 14–16 km, about 6–8 hours, with a pass at roughly 1,760–1,850 m. The Peaks of the Balkans stage page rates it as moderate, with about 1,175 m of ascent. The practical season is June to September. Carry enough water, wear shoes with good grip and bring clothing for changing weather. Before you set off, arrange how you will return or continue, because there is no standardised timetable.

## Safety and respect for the park

The emergency number is 112, but in remote areas the response may be slow. Tell your host about your route, do not set out alone in unsettled weather and do not leave rubbish. We did not find official park rules published on the AKZM page, so ask your host or the park administration.
